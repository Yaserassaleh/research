AUM USD 1.7bn (May 2025) is a press-reported company figure; homepage counters (staff 200, 2025 trading volume USD 43.7bn, 230 research stocks) were read from page markup. Has Arqaam Wealth (HNW/family office, discretionary and advisory), three own funds and an Islamic window; Arqaam Digital page 404.
