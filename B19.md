# Batch B19: Qatar large banks, QNB and QIB (12 Oct 2026)

- **Done:** qa-qnb (Complete, 16 people, 29 sources) and qa-qib (Complete, 11 people, 19 sources). Both files are valid JSON with no broken source references.
- **Financials:** Balance sheets come from the reviewed 30 Jun 2026 statements and FY2025 income from the audited statements. All were filed on QSE and downloaded through its financial-statement API. The primary statements are scanned images and were read visually.
- **QNB Q3 2026:** QNB has already published Q3 2026 results (8 Oct 2026; total assets QAR 1,463.8bn, 9M net profit QAR 13.3bn). These are recorded as extra records. QIB Q3 2026 is not out yet (QSE returned 404).
- **QNB, original extract confirmed:** all four people match the official executive page (CEO Al-Khalifa, Abdulla Hashim Al-Sada for AWM, Al-Malki for Retail, Khalid Al-Sada for C&IB), and so do assets and deposits.
- **QNB, new findings:**
  - The Chairman is Ali bin Ahmed Al-Kuwari.
  - Asset management AUM was QAR 29bn at end-2025.
  - Third-party funds are sourced through Allfunds Bank, and QNBFS offers global trading through Interactive Brokers.
  - QNBFS plans to launch a new integrated retail trading platform in 2026.
- **QNB, lead correction:** "QNB Asset Management" is a division, not a legal entity.
- **QIB, original extract confirmed:** Greg Ellison joined QIB UK as CEO in April 2026. QInvest is a 49% associate; it is linked to qa-qinvest and its data is not duplicated here.
- **QIB, corrections:**
  - The deposits figure of QAR 148.3bn is current accounts plus participatory investment accounts; the file now states this definition.
  - The Annual Report 2025 executive titles differ from the website titles.
  - QIA's stake is 16.67% (FY2025) versus 16.4% (30 Jun 2026).
  - The USD estimates from the original extract were removed.
- **QIB, other findings:** the only "AUM" QIB discloses is QAR 10.5m held off the balance sheet. Investing in the app is limited to QSE stock trading through The Group Securities.
